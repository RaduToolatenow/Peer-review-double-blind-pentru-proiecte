Terms come from `docs/brief.md`. Update them with the words the professor and colleagues use.

**Assignment** (noun) — a rubric sent to a class, with deadlines and k (RO: temă, grilă activă). D-013.
_Avoid_: task, homework

**Rubric** (noun) — the evaluation criteria, their level descriptors and weights (RO: grilă). A professor's own record. A draft (RO: schiță) can be edited; sending it to a class locks it; once used it is archived, not deleted (D-011, D-013).
_Avoid_: form, questionnaire

**Class** (noun) — a professor's group of students, with teams entered from paper (RO: clasă). D-012.
_Avoid_: course, group

**Team code** (noun) — the short code a professor hands to a team; a student enters it once to join (RO: codul echipei). D-012.
_Avoid_: password, invite

**Assignment link** (noun) — the sign-up link a professor shares, /inscriere/<code>; whoever confirms an account through it becomes a reviewer of that assignment (RO: linkul temei). D-010, D-020.
_Avoid_: invite

**Criterion** (noun) — one weighted dimension of a rubric (RO: criteriu).
_Avoid_: question

**Level descriptor** (noun) — the text that defines one level of a criterion (RO: descriptor).
_Avoid_: label

**Submission** (noun) — what a student hands in for an assignment: a link and a file (RO: predare).
_Avoid_: upload, entry

**Author** (noun) — the student who made a submission.
_Avoid_: owner, creator

**Reviewer** (noun) — the student who reviews a submission (RO: evaluator).
_Avoid_: grader, judge

**Review** (noun) — one reviewer's scores and required text feedback on one submission (RO: evaluare).
_Avoid_: rating, assessment

**Allocation** (noun) — the assignment of reviewers to submissions (RO: alocare).
_Avoid_: matching, distribution

**k** (noun) — the number of reviews each submission must receive.
_Avoid_: quota

**Withdrawal** (noun) — a student leaving after allocation, which triggers minimal reallocation (RO: retragere).
_Avoid_: dropout, cancellation

**Calibration** (noun) — the professor reviewing a subset, used to measure reviewers (RO: calibrare).
_Avoid_: benchmark

**Reliability** (noun) — how much a reviewer's scores count, derived from calibration and peer medians (RO: fiabilitate).
_Avoid_: trust score, karma

**Aggregation** (noun) — computing a submission's final grade from its reviews (RO: agregare).
_Avoid_: averaging

**Reviewer grade** (noun) — a student's grade for how well they reviewed (RO: notă de evaluator).
_Avoid_: reviewer score

**Dispute** (noun) — an author's argued challenge to a review, decided by the professor (RO: contestație).
_Avoid_: appeal, complaint

**Phase** (noun) — one server-enforced stage of an assignment: submission, review or dispute (RO: fază).
_Avoid_: stage, round
